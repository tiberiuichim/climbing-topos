## Key beta captured in the topo

- **R1/2** is a provisional junction where Canguru Șchiop's traverse crosses Babilonului's
  long right-traverse (L2, >50 m). No direct connection from Canguru's R1 to that belay.
- **Orientation trap:** the belay under the big roof (R2 Bab. / R3 Cang.) sits 3 m above and
  2 m right of the *Traseul Tinereții* belay — do not drift onto it.
- **Canguru L1:** the crux bulge (7-/7+) — the bolt above the bulge is invisible from below,
  attack straight up, do not deviate left.
- **R4 Bab. / R5 Cang.** is a common summit belay shared with Soroș, Tinereții, Eva,
  Trandafirul Sălbatic.
- Rope: 50–60 m (half ropes help with the L2 friction), 15–18 draws plus long slings for
  Babilonului; 50 m + ~20 short draws for Canguru Șchiop in a party of two.
